Common questions

Quick answers before you apply.

Do candidates have to pay?

No. Searching and applying for jobs is free. Our placement fee is charged only to hiring companies. Premium membership (₹99) is an optional extra for candidates who want us to find jobs for them.

Who pays your fee?

Our placement fee is charged only to the hiring company, as a percentage of the candidate’s annual CTC, invoiced on successful joining. See the commercial terms above.

Which cities do you cover?

We serve employers and candidates across India from our office in SAS Nagar (Mohali), Punjab.

How soon will I hear back?

We review each profile and contact you when a suitable role matches.

I am a fresher. Can I apply?

Yes. Choose Fresher under experience and add your skills.
